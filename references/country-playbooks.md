# Country playbooks

One section per country: where the training money sits, which registers already list providers, seed organisations per lens, local search phrases, how people answer cold email. Only Latvia is verified. The other sections are **starters**: hypotheses from memory to hand to `playbook-workflow.js` as `hints`, not facts to put in an email. After a playbook run, replace the starter with the verified result and date it.

## Latvia (verified 2026-10-07; run completed: 50 vetted, 44 emailed)

**Angle and language:** English. Hook: "entrepreneurs I talk with in Latvia keep asking for AI training in English for their international teams". Base line: say where you are based.

**Funding instruments**
- ESF+ measure 4.2.4.1 "Atbalsts nozaru vajadzībās balstītai pieaugušo izglītībai": 13 sector-association projects (ICT by LIKTA, a second ICT by Latvijas Datortehnoloģiju asociācija, business services ABSL Latvia, machinery MASOC, electronics LETERA, wood, light industry, hotels, printing, windows and doors, chemistry and pharmacy, food, energy); round 2 funds sector skills funds such as POLIGONS (LDA and VATP, 2026 to 2029, 95% public, foreign providers allowed). Employers get 70/60/50% back by company size under de minimis, only for courses bought from the association's procured providers. CFLA pages and the associations' sites publish the providers. Source: likta.lv/esf-digitalo_prasmju-projekts and cfla.gov.lv.
- LIKTA ESF+ project 4.2.4.1/1/24/A/011 (to 31.12.2029): seven procured providers: BDA, DZC, FITA, Komercizglītības centrs, Runas skola, Sadales tīkls, TDL School. Foreign lecturers allowed (MK 413 point 20); a provider can add a lecturer once LIKTA approves.
- ABSL Latvia ESF+ project 4.2.4.1/1/24/A/007: catalogue with 7 providers (SSE Riga, Magnetic Professional, Training Lab, Mercuri, FranklinCovey, KIC, Triviums), each with a named contact. Richest single source.
- MASOC ESF+ lot for AI: Komunikāciju darbnīca.
- EDIC Latvija (EDIH run by the Latvian IT Cluster, dih.lv) RRF 100% services ENDED 30.06.2026; LIKTA's RRF project also ended. DAOL 2 AI (Digital Accelerator of Latvia, Aug 2026 to Jul 2029, EUR 2.57M, 14 partners incl. VATP, MILA, Valmiera Development Agency, Greentech Latvia, LBTU) is building an expert pool. Never say "100% funded".
- Tenders: LIKTA/IEP/2025/10 (leadership, EUR 96k, closed 16.10.2026) shows how associations buy: direct bidding needs Latvian delivery or paid translation, 2 Latvian references, a turnover test, .edoc signature. Partnering with a listed provider is faster.

**Existing lists:** LIKTA provider page; ABSL catalogue page; CFLA 4.2.4.1 project list; eformsb.pvs.iub.gov.lv e-procurement notices with "apmācības"; dih.lv partner list; LTRK training calendar.

**Seeds per lens:** Tier A: SSE Riga Exec Ed, ABSL Latvia, RTU RBS, Trainify, Mercuri, KIC, Latvian IT Cluster EDIH, FranklinCovey, Squalio, NobleProg, Adros, Triviums, LUMA, AI Master Lab, Magnetic Professional.

**Search phrases (LV):** "mākslīgā intelekta kursi uzņēmumiem", "MI apmācības darbiniekiem", "ChatGPT kursi uzņēmumiem Rīga", "pārdošanas apmācības uzņēmumiem", "vadītāju apmācības Rīga", "mārketinga kursi uzņēmumiem", "korporatīvās apmācības", "apmācību centrs uzņēmumiem", "ESF+ 4.2.4.1 apmācību sniedzēji".

**Outreach notes:** English is normal in business email; first names after "Dear"; small providers' CEOs answer within the hour (FITA: 22 minutes); universities and chambers take days; info@ boxes auto-reply. Named person in to, general box in cc.

**Competitor notes:** AI Academy, AI Master Lab, Digital Journey, Magnetic Professional, Squalio and KIC sell AI training themselves; pitch English delivery and Gemini/Copilot depth to them. BDA already has three Copilot courses.

**Holds and relationships:** check who among the EDIH and DAoL 2 AI partners is linked to a relationship you or a partner already own before writing to them.

**Outcome:** 44 emailed on 2026-10-07; one provider CEO replied in 22 minutes and a call was fixed for two days later; an EDIH auto-replied "within 3 working days"; an executive-education director was out of office. Follow-ups after one week.

## Italy (starter, unverified)

Angle and language: Italian; hook = a hands-on practitioner with partner credentials who fills the AI gap in their catalogue; no "English" hook. Base line: say where you are based. Money to check: Fondi interprofessionali (Fondimpresa, Fondirigenti, Fondo For.Te, Fon.Coop, Fonarcom, Fondoprofessioni and others: conto formazione and avvisi, who may be soggetto attuatore, how a freelance docente is contracted by an ente); regional FSE+ catalogues (Lombardia, Lazio, Emilia-Romagna, Veneto, Piemonte) and the enti accreditati registers per region; Fondo Nuove Competenze editions; Formazione 4.0 / Transizione 5.0 credits; Camere di Commercio voucher digitali and PID; ITS Academy; Confindustria Digital Innovation Hubs. Seeds to verify: the big enti (e.g. Cegos Italia, Festo Academy, Galgano, Niuko, Quadrifor, Formaper, 24ORE Business School, Il Sole 24 ORE Formazione, SDA Bocconi exec ed, POLIMI GSoM, LUISS Business School, CUOA, MIB Trieste, Bologna Business School), regional training consortia, the AI-specific players (e.g. Talent Garden, Datapizza, AI Academy by various agencies), sales and leadership firms (Mercuri Italia, FranklinCovey Italia, Dale Carnegie Italia). Search phrases (IT): "formazione finanziata fondi interprofessionali enti", "ente di formazione accreditato Regione", "corsi intelligenza artificiale per aziende", "formazione AI aziendale", "docente esterno ente di formazione", "academy aziendale".
